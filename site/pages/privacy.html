This policy explains what pintsplit.com and the Split The G app ("the site", "we", "us") collect, why, who else is involved, and the choices you have. Split The G is an independent project run by one person. If anything here is unclear, email bryansable52@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The camera is used only on your phone to find your glass and the liquid line. Camera frames are never uploaded.
  • You can score without an account. Your scores are then kept only in your browser.
  • If you sign in, we store your email address, username and scores so they follow you between devices and appear to your friends.
  • The site shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads, including ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised ads.

Information we collect

Camera

When you score a pint, the app asks for permission to use your camera. Video frames are analysed in your browser, on your device, to find the rim, base and liquid line of your glass. Frames are not recorded, stored on our servers or sent to us. The snapshot shown with your result stays on your device unless you choose to share it, in which case it goes wherever you send it.

Scores and history

Your scores, the time you recorded them and your settings (such as the pub or sparkling edition and your answer to the age question) are stored in your browser's local storage. Clearing your browser data deletes them.

Accounts (optional)

If you sign in, we collect your email address so we can send you a sign-in link, and the username you choose. Your scores are then also stored in our database so they sync across devices and can appear on your friends' leaderboards and the daily G-off board. Your username is visible to people you add as friends and to anyone who searches for it to add you. Our database and sign-in emails are provided by Supabase.

Technical information

Like almost every website, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) receives standard request information such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, and may keep it in server logs for security and operations. The site loads its display font from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers. We do not run a separate analytics service and do not build profiles of visitors ourselves. If we add an analytics tool in the future, we will update this policy first.

Cookies, local storage and advertising

The site itself sets no tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to keep your scores, your sign-in session and your preferences.

We use Google AdSense to show advertising, which helps pay for running the site. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. These vendors may also use cookies or similar technologies to measure ad performance and to limit how often you see an ad.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info or, in Europe, youronlinechoices.eu.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalised advertising cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the game will keep working, though ads may become less relevant.

How we use information

  • To run the game: scoring, history, friends, challenges and the daily leaderboard.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To answer messages you send us.
  • To show advertising that keeps the site free.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your camera images for anything other than scoring your sip on your device.

Service providers

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site.
  • Supabase stores account and score data for signed-in users and sends sign-in emails.
  • Google provides advertising (AdSense) and web fonts (Google Fonts).

These providers may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own privacy policies and safeguards.

How long we keep data

Account data and scores are kept until you ask us to delete them. Data in your browser stays until you clear it. Emails you send us are kept as long as needed to deal with your message.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To make a request, including deleting your account and scores, email us at the address below. We'll respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Age

The pub edition is a game about beer and is intended only for people of legal drinking age where they live. The sparkling edition uses sparkling water and is open to everyone. The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.
