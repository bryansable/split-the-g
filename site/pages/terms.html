What the service is

Split The G is a free game that uses your device's camera to estimate how close the liquid line in your glass is to where the G sits on a classic pint glass, and gives you a score. Scores are an estimate for entertainment only. Lighting, angles and glass shapes affect readings, and a score is not a measurement you should rely on for anything else.

Age and responsible play

  • The pub edition involves alcohol and is only for people of legal drinking age in the country where they live. The sparkling edition uses sparkling water and anyone may play it.
  • The game is about a single sip. Don't turn it into a drinking game, don't pressure anyone to drink, and never drink and drive.
  • You are responsible for your own drinking and your own safety. Please follow your local health guidance and drink responsibly.
