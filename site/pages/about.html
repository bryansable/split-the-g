Split The G is a small, independent web app that turns a classic pub challenge into a score. You take one sip from your pint, point your phone at the glass, and the app tells you how close the line came to the middle of the G, to the millimetre.

Why it exists

Anyone who has tried to split the G with friends knows how it goes. Someone lands it, or thinks they did, and the whole table leans in to argue about whether the line is dead centre or a hair high. It's half the fun, but it never gets settled. I wanted a neutral judge that lives in your pocket, works with whatever glass the pub hands you, and gives everyone the same honest number.

Getting that right turned out to be an interesting problem. The app has to find the rim and base of a glass in a dim pub, read the line between beer and head at the front of the glass where the G is printed, and do it all on your phone without sending your camera feed anywhere. It measures everything as a fraction of the glass height, so it doesn't matter how big the glass is or how far away you hold the phone. You can read more about that in how the app scores your sip.

What we care about

  • One sip, not more. The game is about a single, well-judged sip. It's never meant to be a drinking game, and the responsible play guide says so plainly.
  • Everyone can play. The sparkling edition is the same game with sparkling water, for drivers, non-drinkers and anyone under drinking age.
  • Your camera stays yours. Frames are analysed on your phone and never uploaded. See the privacy policy.
  • Free to play. There's no app to buy and no account needed to score. Ads on the site help cover hosting and development.

The blog

Alongside the app, the blog covers the things people actually ask about: how to land the sip, why stout is poured in two parts, why the head is so creamy, how to host a G-off with friends, and how to photograph a pint so the score (and the photo) come out right.
