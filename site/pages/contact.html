Helpful things to include

  • Bug reports: your phone model, your browser (Safari, Chrome and so on) and what happened. A screenshot helps a lot.
  • A glass the app can't read: a photo of the glass on a plain background, and what the app did with it.
  • Feature ideas: anything that would make your G-offs better.
  • Account or data requests: the email address you signed in with and what you'd like done, for example deleting your account and scores. See the privacy policy for your rights.

Before you write

The FAQ on the home page answers the most common questions, and how the app scores your sip explains the readings in detail. If the app is struggling to find your glass, how to photograph a pint has tips on light, angle and background.
